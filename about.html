About Primeghost Technology

We started Primeghost with a simple idea: businesses shouldn't have to choose between technology that works and technology they can understand.

Our Mission

To give businesses of every size access to reliable, well-built technology — websites, software, and security — without the jargon or the inflated price tag that often comes with it.

Our Vision

To be the IT partner businesses turn to first, known for work that's built properly the first time and maintained honestly after launch.

Our Values

Clarity

We explain what we're building and why, in language that makes sense to you, not just to other developers.

Accountability

If something breaks, we own it. Every project includes support after launch, not just a handover email.

Craft

We'd rather build one thing well than five things quickly. Clean code and tested systems save everyone time later.

How we work

Every engagement starts with listening — understanding what your business actually needs before we talk about technology at all. From there we scope the work clearly, keep you updated as we build, and test thoroughly before anything goes live.

Who we work with

Small and growing businesses that need a website, a custom tool, or a second opinion on their IT security — and want a team that treats their project like it matters, because it does.
