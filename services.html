Our Services

Four areas of work, each one covering a real gap most small and growing businesses run into sooner or later.

Web Development

We design and build websites that load quickly, work on every device, and are genuinely easy to update afterwards. That includes everything from a simple business site to a more complex site with forms, bookings, or a content section.

  • Custom-built, responsive websites
  • Search-engine-friendly structure and page speed
  • Ongoing maintenance and updates
WD

Best for

Businesses that need a professional online presence or an existing site that's slow, outdated, or hard to manage.

Cybersecurity

We assess where your systems are exposed and put practical protections in place — before an incident forces the issue. This covers everything from basic security hygiene to a full audit of how customer data is handled.

  • Security audits and risk assessments
  • Data protection practices for customer information
  • Staff guidance on safe digital habits
CS

Best for

Businesses handling customer data, payments, or sensitive records who want to close obvious gaps.

Software Solutions

When an off-the-shelf tool doesn't quite fit, we build something that does — internal tools, booking systems, dashboards, or anything else your business needs to run more smoothly day to day.

  • Custom software built around your workflow
  • Integration with tools you already use
  • Testing and support after delivery
SS

Best for

Teams working around the limits of generic software, or repeating manual work that could be automated.

IT Consulting

Sometimes the most useful thing we can do is give you a clear, honest read on your options before you spend anything. We help with technology decisions, infrastructure planning, and setting priorities that actually match your budget.

  • Technology strategy and planning
  • Infrastructure and tooling recommendations
  • Second opinions on existing systems or vendor proposals
IT

Best for

Businesses about to make a technology decision and wanting an independent perspective first.
